WHAT IS THE SCOOPAVELI LEAGUE?

The Scoopaveli League is a women's athlete development, visibility, and recognition ecosystem.

The league was created to give women a structured environment where they can build their personal brand, increase visibility, participate in competitive scouting cycles, and pursue advancement opportunities within the Scoopaveli system.

Unlike traditional social media platforms, the Scoopaveli League is built around participation, rankings, evaluation, and recognition.

Athletes enter Scoopaveli University, where they complete challenges, submit content, participate in scouting activities, build rankings, and compete for visibility opportunities throughout the ecosystem.

Top-performing athletes may earn featured placements, athlete spotlights, ranking movement, draft consideration opportunities, and additional recognition within the league.
